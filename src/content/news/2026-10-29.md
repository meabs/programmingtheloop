  - vendor: "OpenAI"
    title: "API credits for ChatGPT Pro and Business"
    releasedOn: "28 Oct 2026"
    body: |
      Paid ChatGPT Pro and Business subscriptions now come with free monthly API credits. Pro gets the price of the plan back as credit ($100, $200 or $500 a month on the three Pro tiers). Business gets $20 per paid Standard seat and $100 per Premium seat, pooled and capped at $500 a month per workspace.

      You turn it on once by linking an API organisation you own (same email as your ChatGPT account, subscription at least seven days old), and it then renews with the subscription. The first grant is prorated and unused credit expires each billing cycle.

      The credit covers Responses, Agents, Decisions, Realtime, Images and built-in tools. It doesn't cover Codex used through API keys and it doesn't raise ChatGPT or Codex limits. The full terms are in the [Help Center article](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001626-api-credits-benefit-for-eligible-chatgpt-subscriptions).
    whoItAffects: "Pro and Business subscribers, who get a monthly budget for API prototypes and agent experiments with no payment card on the API account. Check the exclusions before relying on it, since it doesn't cover Codex used through API keys."
    sourceUrl: "https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/chatgpt-api-credits"
    sourceLabel: "OpenAI API credits guide"

  - vendor: "Google"
    title: "Faster GPU inference with LiteRT in Google Play services"
    releasedOn: "28 Oct 2026"
    body: |
      Android's shared on-device runtime for custom models now runs GPU inference about 1.4x faster than the old GPU delegate, through the new CompiledModel API.

      Because the runtime ships with Play services instead of inside your APK, Google says apps that switch come out about 8 MB smaller and pick up fixes automatically. There's an Android Studio LiteRT skill to help migrate.
    whoItAffects: "Android teams shipping on-device models, who can reduce app size and pick up faster GPU inference without bundling their own runtime."
    sourceUrl: "https://developers.googleblog.com/smaller-apps-and-faster-gpu-inference-with-litert-in-google-play-services/"

    releasedOn: "23 Oct 2026"
    body: |
      Claude Enterprise organisations get `/v1/organizations/claude_tag/` endpoints, behind the `experimental-claude-tag-2026-10-21` beta header, to read and change Claude Tag settings on Slack channels and workspaces.
