## Research question

Can minimal repository guidance improve a coding agent’s ability to discover authoritative external dependencies and recognise when required information is unavailable?

## Hypothesis

Minimal signposting may help an agent find authoritative dependency information. An explicit stop instruction may also reduce unsupported answers when required information cannot be found. These are proposals to test, not findings.

## Proposed comparison

1. **Baseline:** no additional signposting.
2. **Minimal signposting:** point to the likely authoritative external dependency.
3. **Signposting plus explicit stop instruction:** provide the pointer and instruct the agent to stop when required authoritative information remains unavailable.

## Proposed setup

The initial plan uses Shopizer-derived material where feasible, two tasks and five repetitions per arm: 30 trial runs. Task definitions, repository state, model, tools, configuration and success criteria remain to be frozen before counted runs.

## Results

Not run. No result, cost, time, model metadata or commit is available for publication.

## Limitations and open questions

The plan is preliminary. Shopizer feasibility, task design, authoritative-source availability and the exact outcome rubric must be resolved and preregistered. Results from two tasks would remain limited to those tasks and conditions.
