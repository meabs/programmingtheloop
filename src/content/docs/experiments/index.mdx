# Experiments

Research pages should distinguish measured findings from recommendations and open questions. Results in the first edition come from small, task-specific trials. Source repositories and files are named in the PDF.

## From the first edition

| Study | Observation | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Repository optimisation (Experiment J) | Prepared Java repository used 35–48% more tokens, without reliable correctness improvement in the tested tasks (29 vs 28 of 32 changes; 19 vs 16 of 24 structure questions). | One repository and model; mechanical edits and structure questions; 8 runs per task per arm; four ingredients combined. |
| Stale dependency knowledge | 14 of 16 planted drift answers repeated old names without hedging. | One model; four runs per question; drift was planted. |
| Missing dependency facts | Hidden-test passes rose from 3 of 20 with no backend facts to 18–19 of 20 with a guide. | One model; five runs per task; identifiers renamed by the author. |
| Verification on the worked example | All 12 runs reported `passed` while the real-service criterion was unverified; without the contract, agents rewrote the freshness gate; with the contract, all 6 runs were correct. | One model and task; 6 runs per arm. |

See [Chapter 3](/learn/right-facts/) and [Chapter 5](/learn/proof-not-promises/) for interpretation. Prefer the [PDF](/downloads/) for full wording and sources.
