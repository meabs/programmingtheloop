# Research methodology

## Evidence labels

- **Measured finding:** A direct observation in a described experiment.
- **Engineering recommendation:** Advice informed by evidence and judgement.
- **Open question:** A hypothesis or uncertainty not established by available results.

## What published findings include

Where the source supports it, pages state the task, repository, model, configuration, repetitions and outcome. Limitations appear beside results. If the first edition identifies evidence paths that are not yet publicly linked, the text names those paths without presenting them as clickable sources.

## Corrections and new evidence

Historical results remain attached to the conditions under which they were collected. Corrections should identify what changed and why. New evidence should be published as a separate record, with source code, run logs, task definitions, commits and deviations linked when available.

The first edition describes small trials and uses specific models and repositories. Its conclusions should not be generalised beyond the stated conditions without further evidence.
