# About

I write here about agentic engineering and coding agents. Most of what is up so far is the free ebook [*Programming the Loop: What I Measured with Coding Agents*](/book/) (first edition, October 2026) and the [worked example](https://github.com/meabs/ProgrammingTheLoop-WorkedExample) that runs through it.

## Me

My experiments on coding agents are published at [github.com/meabs](https://github.com/meabs). I share practical work on frugal, local-first and evidence-based AI engineering on YouTube as [Frugal AI Engineer](https://www.youtube.com/@FrugalAIEngineer), and on [X as @Mav1972](https://x.com/Mav1972). I live in the UK with my wife, Mrs Meabs, and our house rabbits, Humphrey and Victor.

## Findings and recommendations

In the book, **findings** are the measured results, each with the file they come from and the limits of the trial. They describe those trials and nothing wider. **Recommendations** are my engineering judgement. Where a recommendation goes beyond what I measured, the text says so.

## Feedback

If something is wrong or unclear, [open an issue](https://github.com/meabs/ProgrammingTheLoop-WorkedExample/issues/new/choose) on the worked example.
