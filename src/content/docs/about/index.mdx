# About

**Programming the Loop: What I Measured with Coding Agents** is a free ebook by Garry Meaburn (first edition, October 2026). This website hosts the PDF, a chapter list, short web notes for each chapter, and room for later notes, experiments and updates that are not only the book.

## Author

Garry Meaburn writes about coding agents and evidence-based AI engineering. Experiments and related repositories are published at [github.com/meabs](https://github.com/meabs). He shares practical work on [YouTube (Frugal AI Engineer)](https://www.youtube.com/@FrugalAIEngineer) and on [X (@Mav1972)](https://x.com/Mav1972). He lives in the UK with his wife, Mrs Meabs, and their house rabbits, Humphrey and Victor.

## Evidence and recommendations

The book distinguishes **findings** (measured results with limits) from **recommendations** (engineering judgement). Web pages here should keep that line. Do not invent numbers, quotes, testimonials or download counts.
