# About

This site is where I write about **agentic build engineering**: building software with coding agents. You'll find [articles](/articles/), [experiments](/experiments/) and [guides](/guides/). *Programming the Loop* is the first guide. The October 2026 PDF is on the [downloads](/downloads/) page if you want that version.

## Me

My experiments on coding agents are published at [github.com/meabs](https://github.com/meabs). I share practical work on frugal, local-first and evidence-based AI engineering on YouTube as [Frugal AI Engineer](https://www.youtube.com/@FrugalAIEngineer), and on [X as @Mav1972](https://x.com/Mav1972). I live in the UK with my wife, Mrs Meabs, and our house rabbits, Humphrey and Victor.

For authorship and evidence disclosures from the book (including who ran the experiments and who wrote the words), see [About the evidence](/guides/programming-the-loop/about-the-evidence/) and [About the author](/guides/programming-the-loop/about-the-author/) in the Guide.

## Findings and recommendations

Where I publish results, **findings** are measured outcomes with limits. **Recommendations** are judgement. Where a recommendation goes beyond what I measured, I say so.
