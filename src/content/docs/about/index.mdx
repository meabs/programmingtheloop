# About

**Programming the Loop** is Garry Meaburn’s site for **agentic engineering**: working with coding agents — practices, measurements, experiments and notes.

The free ebook *[Programming the Loop: What I Measured with Coding Agents](/book/)* (first edition, October 2026) is the main published material so far. Notes and experiments will appear in their own sections when there is something to publish.

## Author

Garry Meaburn writes about coding agents and evidence-based AI engineering. Related repositories are at [github.com/meabs](https://github.com/meabs). He shares practical work on [YouTube (Frugal AI Engineer)](https://www.youtube.com/@FrugalAIEngineer) and on [X (@Mav1972)](https://x.com/Mav1972). He lives in the UK with his wife, Mrs Meabs, and their house rabbits, Humphrey and Victor.

## What exists today

- [The book](/book/) — free PDF and chapter notes
- [Worked example](https://github.com/meabs/ProgrammingTheLoop-WorkedExample) — repository used through the book
- [Feedback](https://github.com/meabs/ProgrammingTheLoop-WorkedExample/issues/new/choose) — open an issue on the worked example

## Evidence and recommendations

Where results are discussed, **findings** (measured, with limits) stay separate from **recommendations** and **open questions**. Do not invent numbers, quotes, testimonials or download counts.
